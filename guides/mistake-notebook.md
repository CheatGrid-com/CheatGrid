# Mistake notebook 📓

Everyone gets things wrong while studying. The trick is to come back to exactly those things, and that's what the mistake notebook is for. It fills itself as you study, so there's nothing to set up.

## What collects there

- **Practice-test questions you got wrong.** Finish a practice test (Practice or Exam mode) and every question you missed lands in your notebook, with the right answer, the explanation and its source.
- **Flashcards you keep forgetting.** Cards you knew once and then forgot more than once show up too, straight from your flashcard memory.

Get a question right later, in a practice test or in the notebook itself, and it **comes off the list**. That's the whole idea: the notebook only holds what you still need to fix.

## Working through it

- **Test me on these:** a short quiz on one topic's open questions, answers shuffled. Right answers come off the list at once; wrong ones stay and count again. Miss one again and **Why was my answer wrong?** explains your pick: why it's tempting, where it breaks, and a check for next time.
- **Practise these cards:** opens a flashcard practice session with exactly the cards you keep forgetting.
- **I've got it now:** take a question off the list yourself when you're sure.
- **Download or print:** save the whole notebook as a Markdown file, or print it for a last look before an exam.

## Where to find it

Open **Mistake notebook** from your account menu or your **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)**. After a practice test, the results screen also tells you how many questions went into it.

You need to be signed in, since the notebook is yours alone. It works on every topic you can study, free ones included.
