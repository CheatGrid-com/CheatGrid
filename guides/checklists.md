# ✅ Checklists

Checklists for the work that actually happens. Each one walks you through a real task, like shipping a feature, running an incident or onboarding a new hire, in the order you meet it, with **what matters most first**.

## What a checklist looks like

The top tells you when you'd open it and how often people in that role meet it, then a short **How to use**. Under that:

- **At a glance** is the one-minute version: every part of the task with all its checks, the **Must** ones first and marked, plus its stop signs. **Click a check** to jump to it in its moment, or Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on a Mac) to tick it right there; the box ticks too. Point at a check to see why it matters and how to verify it.
- **The moments**, in the order you meet them. Each one starts with a line on where you are, then its **Must** checks, then **Don't forget these too**. Those are needed as well, never optional; they just come second.
- A red **Stop if** box says when to stop and who to ask, and **Move on when** says when that part is done.
- Every check has a line under it saying why it matters, and **How to verify** says what to look at to know it's really done.
- Where an official or expert page states a check, a **small number in a circle** after its title opens that page. It's the same number as in **Sources** at the end. Checks without a circle are plain know-how.
- At the end: what this checklist doesn't cover, related checklists, the cheat sheets behind it, a **map of similar checklists**, and the sources.
- The **similar checklists map** puts this checklist in the middle and the checklists closest to it in what they cover around it, from any roadmap. **Closer and bigger means more alike**, each roadmap has its own colour, and every dot opens its checklist. On a phone it's a list.

## Your case

Some checklists ask **up to two questions** before you start, like what kind of system you're working on. Some let you pick **any that apply**. Checks that don't fit your answers drop away, and an **Only if** chip on a check shows which answer it depends on (tap it to jump to the question). The page keeps your answers in its link, so you can share a checklist set up for your case.

## Search inside a checklist

Type a word in the search bar above At a glance and the page shows only the checks that mention it. Small typos are fine. Every match lights up, and a match inside a closed section opens it. Press Escape or the × to see everything again. Printing and copying always give you the whole checklist.

## Finding one

- **In the menu:** on a computer, Checklists sit in the top menu right after Concept Maps. On a phone, they're in the bottom bar, where Life Skills used to be. Life Skills is now in the ☰ menu.
- **On the [Checklists](https://www.cheatgrid.com/checklists) page:** three shelves, **Careers**, **Skills** and **Life**, each with a **Try free** row listing its free checklists. Every roadmap has its own list, including checklists it shares with other roadmaps: each roadmap is a box with its checklist count, and **pointing at it** shows its checklists (on a phone, tapping opens the list).
- **On a roadmap page:** the **Checklists** line at the top and the small pill on a step **show their checklists when you point at them**. On a phone, tapping opens the list page.
- **On a cheat sheet:** a line points to the checklists that use it.
- **In search:** pick the **Checklists** filter to search titles and checks.

Can't find the one you need? **[Request a checklist](requests.md)** and tell us the task.

## Ticking it off

- **Your ticks are saved to your account** when you're signed in. Signed out, they're saved on this device; sign in to keep them everywhere, and they move into your account the first time you do.
- **Tick all the small items under a check and the check ticks itself**, and ticking the check ticks all its items.
- **Only Must** shows just the Must checks and stop signs, for when time is short. Your count doesn't change.
- Everything starts open. **Fold all** at the top tucks away the extra checks and every "How to verify" (**Open all** brings them back), and the page remembers your last choice on that device.
- **Mark this moment done** ticks a whole part at once. A part marked **every time** has a **Start again** button, for steps you repeat.
- You've **finished** a checklist when every check that applies to your answers is ticked. **Start fresh** clears it for the next time, and the page counts how many times you've finished it.
- If we improve a checklist after you've used it, a note tells you which of your ticks no longer match.

## Use it offline in the app

In the installed CheatGrid app, press **Save offline** on any checklist you can open and it stays on that device, up to 10 of them. With no connection, the app's offline page lists them and you can tick as you go; **your ticks are saved when you're back online**. Paid checklists stay offline while your plan is active, and signing out removes them from the device.

## Print, copy or save as PDF

- **Print** gives you the whole checklist with every section open.
- **Copy as text** turns it into a to-do list you can paste into Notion, Obsidian or a ticket.
- The **PDF** button: if you're signed in and can open the checklist, you get the **whole checklist as a clean file**, counted toward the same daily PDF allowance as cheat sheets and Life Skills. Otherwise you get a watermarked preview of what you can see, with a last page saying what the full file holds.

## What's free

**Nine whole checklists are free** for everyone: one in each of our most popular roadmaps, and one in every field none of those covers. They wear a green **Free** label and sit in the **Try free** row. Every other checklist needs **Pro**. A locked checklist shows **the first check of each moment in full**, and its At a glance lists each moment with that first check and how many more it holds, so you can see what you'd get.

## What you earn

You earn XP for every minute you spend on a checklist you can open, a little for each check you tick, a bonus when you finish a part, more for finishing the whole checklist, a smaller bonus when you finish it again on another day, and a big one for finishing every checklist of a roadmap. Badges come with milestones. See **[XP & Levels](xp-and-levels.md)**.

A day counts for your **[streak](streaks.md)** once you earn **10 XP from checklists**: ten ticked checks, ten minutes on a checklist, finishing one, or any mix.

## Getting more detail

**Summarize** at the top explains the whole checklist in plain words. The small **Explain** icon on a moment or a check walks you through that one part: why it matters and how to do it. The buttons are there for **everyone**, signed in or not, on everything you can see: all of a free checklist, and the moments and first checks of a locked one (Summarize needs the whole checklist, so it appears once you can open it). They use **the same allowance** as every other Explain button on the site; signed out, the panel asks you to sign in first. More in **[AI Explanations](ai-explanations.md)**.

## How it ties to everything else

Every checklist belongs to a **[roadmap](roadmaps.md)** and shows up on the roadmaps of other roles that meet the same task. It links to the **[cheat sheets](cheat-sheets.md)** behind its checks, and through them to their recipes. Your ticks show up on your **[Stats](stats.md)** page and your **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)**.

## Tell us how it's doing

At the end of every checklist there are quick feedback buttons:

- 👍 **Good quality** or 👎 **Bad quality** to rate it at a glance
- 🔄 **Update needed** if something looks out of date
- 💬 **Leave a note** to say what you'd change

We read them all, and they decide what gets fixed and extended next.
