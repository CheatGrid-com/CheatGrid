# Dashboard

Your **Dashboard** is the one-page read on your own learning: what you have built up so far, how you have been studying lately, and what is worth doing next. Open it any time from your account menu.

Everything on it is **your** numbers only. There are no leaderboards and no comparisons with other people.

## How the page is laid out

The page is grouped by **what each number is about**, and every group has its own colour: cheat sheets are blue, practice tests orange, flashcards green, cookbooks pink, and violet is used for the parts that cover all four at once. The strip of buttons at the top jumps you straight to a group, and the dots on those buttons are the colour key.

### Overview (violet)

- **Next up:** cards due for review right now, the last cheat sheet you had open, a recipe you left half done, and whether today already counts towards your **[streak](streaks.md)**.
- **Headline numbers:** the first row is your totals across everything (XP and level, time studied, best streak, roadmaps). The second row is one number per kind of content: cheat sheets read, cards reviewed, tests completed, recipes finished.
- **Level progress and where the XP came from:** one bar per source, so you can see at a glance whether your XP is coming from reading, testing, cards, recipes or roadmaps.
- **Where your time goes** and **what earns your streak days.**

### Cheat sheets (blue)

- **Cheat sheets you have read most:** by time spent on the sheet itself. Time on its deck or its tests is counted in those sections instead, so this is genuinely reading time.
- **Concept Maps:** how many sheets you've explored on the **[Concept Maps](concept-maps.md)**, how many maps you've touched, your time there, and where you've explored most, with the next milestone.
- **You've never opened these:** the most connected cheat sheets in an area you study that you haven't touched yet, taken from the **[Concept Maps](concept-maps.md)**. The ones that link to something you already know come first. Pick another area from the menu on the panel; your choice is remembered in this browser.
- **What you save most** and **saved rows worth re-checking:** your **[My Vault](my-vault.md)** rows, and which sheets have been updated since you saved from them.

### Practice tests (orange)

- **Exam readiness:** for each certification you've studied, your readiness number and verdict. Tap one to see it by exam domain, with what to study next (more in the **[Certifications guide](certifications.md)**).
- **Score spread:** how your best scores are distributed, your average best, plus a short "worth another look" list of the ones sitting under 70%.
- **Proved it without the safety net:** Practice mode tells you after each question; Exam mode does not. This shows which tests you have cleared cold.
- **Since your best run:** tests where your latest attempt came in well under your best. Your best score is what counts everywhere else, so this is just a nudge, not a mark against you.
- **Half finished:** subjects where you have done some of the tests but not the rest.

### Flashcards and memory (green)

- **Study rhythm:** a calendar grid of the last 12 weeks, shaded by how many cards you reviewed each day, plus the shape of a typical session.
- **When you recall best:** how often you press Good or Easy, broken down by the hour of day you were studying. Most people have a clear best hour and are surprised by which one it is. Hours you have barely studied are left blank rather than guessed at.
- **Your week:** the same thing by day of the week.
- **What is coming, next 14 days:** how many cards are expected to come up each day. Everything else on the page looks backwards; this one looks ahead, and it is the easiest way to spot a busy patch before it arrives.
- **How your recall is going:** the mix of Again / Hard / Good / Easy you gave yourself in recent sessions.
- **Memory across your decks:** how much you have learned and are not currently due, roughly how long a typical card of yours now sticks, and an estimate of how much you would recall if tested today.
- **How settled your cards are:** the split between still learning, young, and mature, plus which decks stick best for you.
- **Cards that keep fighting you:** the handful you have forgotten most often. Usually a card like this needs a different angle rather than more repetitions, so reading its row on the cheat sheet again tends to sort it out faster than drilling does.
- **Decks you have worked hardest** and **decks that have gone quiet:** nothing is lost or deleted, the quiet ones are simply the decks you have not opened in a while.

### Cookbooks (pink)

Once you have tried at least one **[recipe](https://www.cheatgrid.com/cookbooks)**, this section shows how you cook:

- **The headline numbers:** how many recipes you have finished out of the ones you started, steps ticked, whole cookbooks finished, cookbook XP earned, and how many roadmaps you have touched.
- **How you cook:** your typical pace from starting a recipe to finishing it, your fastest finish, and roughly how many hours of reading you have put in across every recipe you have completed.
- **Skill level:** a split of your finished recipes by beginner, intermediate and advanced.
- **Where you have been cooking:** the roadmaps you have touched and your most-visited category.
- **This week:** how many recipes you finished in the last 7 days, plus a small strip showing which days you were busy and which days you actually finished something.
- **Pick up where you left off, closest to a badge, and recently finished:** the same shortcuts the Cookbooks hub gives you, right here on your dashboard.

If you have not tried a recipe yet, this section stays out of the way with one line pointing you at the **[Cookbooks](https://www.cheatgrid.com/cookbooks)** page instead of a wall of zeroes.

### Everything together (violet)

- **Your biggest subjects:** total time on each, with each bar split by where the time went. It is the one chart that puts reading, tests and cards side by side, so you can see whether you mostly read a subject or mostly drilled it.
- **Where you spend your time:** the same hours grouped by category.
- **Subjects with more to give:** a subject here is a cheat sheet plus its flashcard deck and its practice test. These are the ones where you have used some of the three but not all, with a link straight to the missing one.
- **Roadmaps in progress** and **your account and tools:** your plan, your **[AI explanation](ai-explanations.md)** allowance, and your **[My Vault](my-vault.md)** size.

## Your share cards, in one place

Every card you have earned collects here: scores from [practice tests](practice-tests.md), finished [roadmaps](roadmaps.md) and [decks](intelligent-flashcards.md), and your [streak](streaks.md). Each one is an image plus a page you can link to, and each names the thing you finished rather than you. Nothing is published unless you copy the link yourself.

## Dashboard or Stats?

Both, for different jobs.

- **Dashboard** is the summary and the "what next": totals, charts, trends, a handful of top things.
- **[Stats](stats.md)** is the full ledger: every topic, deck and test, sortable, searchable, with your own counters and notes.

## A note on refreshing

The Dashboard takes a **snapshot** when you open it and then holds still. It does not tick along while you leave the tab open. The header always tells you how old the snapshot is, and there is a **Refresh** button when you want a fresh one, for instance right after a study session.
