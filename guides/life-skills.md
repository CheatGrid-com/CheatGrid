# 💡 Life Skills

Life Skills takes something you probably already believe about work, money, relationships or everyday decisions, and shows you where it's wrong, with the source. Not more advice you've already heard, and not a life hack. One page, a few minutes, and you leave knowing something specific was wrong and why.

## What a page looks like

Every page opens with the situation it's about and the goal, what you'll get out of reading it, then a checklist for the moment you're actually in it and the three biggest things up front, so you get the point even if you stop there. Below that, the full set of findings, grouped by what part of the situation they touch, each one labelled with what it is (do, don't, watch, rule, know, say, myth), paired with the reasoning behind it, and carrying the source it came from. Most pages end with a short list of related myths worth retiring, each one paired with what's actually true.

## Finding one

Everything is organized into life areas, and each life area into topics. Browse from the **Life Skills** tab in the main nav: every life area shows its topics straight away, and on a mouse, hovering a topic peeks at the pages inside it without leaving the list. Topic titles are real links, so you can go straight to the full list of pages instead, and opening a life area lays out its topics with every page listed under them. The search box narrows all of it to what matches as you type.

Above all of that, the green **Latest** stripe at the top of the page lists the newest pages, newest first. When you're signed in, each one says whether you've read it yet.

## How much you can try

Every topic gives you one full page to read for free, no account needed. Past that, you'll still see what every other page in that topic covers, just not the findings themselves, until you subscribe.

## What you get for reading

XP for every page you mark as read, more once you've finished every page in a topic, and more again once you've finished every topic in a life area. A few minutes of actual reading also earns the day's streak point, the same as reading a cheat sheet.

## Getting more detail

Want a single finding explained in plainer language, or the reasoning behind a whole group spelled out? Tap **Explain** on it for a plain-language walkthrough.

## How it ties to everything else

Your reading counts toward your **[streak](streaks.md)** and your **[XP](xp-and-levels.md)**, and shows up on your **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)** and your **[Stats](stats.md)** page alongside everything else you've worked through.
