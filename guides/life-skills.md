# 💡 Life Skills

Life Skills pages are cheat sheets for everyday life: work, money, relationships, family and the big changes. Every page gives you two things.

- **A cheat sheet, for what you didn't know.** Short lines on what really holds true in that situation, each with its source and a sentence on why.
- **A checklist, for what you'd forget.** The steps in order, often with the exact words to say, and what to do if it doesn't go to plan.

Most of the steps you'll half know already, and that's fine. Pilots and surgeons use checklists for things they know by heart, because it's easy to skip a step when it counts. Read the cheat sheet once, and come back to the checklist whenever you need it.

## What a page looks like

Every page opens with the situation it's about and the goal, then the checklist and the three biggest things up front, so you get the point even if you stop there. Below that is the cheat sheet itself: the full set of findings, grouped by what part of the situation they touch, each one labelled with what it is (do, don't, watch, rule, know, say, myth), paired with the reasoning behind it, and carrying the source it came from. Most pages end with a short list of related myths worth retiring, each one paired with what's actually true.

## Finding one

Everything is organized into life areas, and each life area into topics. Browse from the **Life Skills** tab in the main nav: every life area shows its topics straight away, and on a mouse, hovering a topic peeks at the pages inside it without leaving the list. Topic titles are real links, so you can go straight to the full list of pages instead, and opening a life area lays out its topics with every page listed under them. The search box narrows all of it to what matches as you type.

Above all of that, the green **Latest** stripe at the top of the page lists the newest pages, newest first. When you're signed in, each one says where you are with it: **Not read yet**, **Opened** once you've been on the page, and **Read** once you've marked it as read at the end.

## How much you can try

Every topic gives you one full page to read for free, no account needed. Past that, you'll still see what every other page in that topic covers and the first step of its checklist, just not the findings or the rest of the list, until you subscribe.

## Take it with you (PDF)

Every page has a **PDF** button at the top, next to the title. Press it and the page comes back as a designed document: the goal, the checklist with real tick boxes, the three biggest things, every finding with its source as a numbered link, the myths, and a printable list of sources at the end. Good for printing the checklist, or keeping a page for a conversation you know is coming.

- **Subscribers get the clean file:** the whole page, no watermark.
- **Everyone else gets a preview file:** the title, the goal, the section headings, one checklist step and (on a free page) the three biggest things, watermarked, with a page at the end explaining what's in the rest. That includes the free pages, so a free page is fully readable on the site, and the take-away file needs **Pro**.

There's a daily limit on downloads so the service stays fast for everyone, and it's the same allowance the cheat-sheet PDFs use.

## What you get for reading

XP for the time you spend reading, the same as on a cheat sheet (it only counts while you're really on the page, and you can watch it climb in the XP bar), plus a bonus for every page you mark as read, more once you've finished every page in a topic, and more again once you've finished every topic in a life area. A few minutes of actual reading also earns the day's streak point.

## Getting more detail

Want a single finding explained in plainer language, or the reasoning behind a whole group spelled out? Tap **Explain** on it for a plain-language walkthrough.

## How it ties to everything else

Your reading counts toward your **[streak](streaks.md)** and your **[XP](xp-and-levels.md)**, and shows up on your **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)** and your **[Stats](stats.md)** page alongside everything else you've worked through.

## Tell us how it's doing

At the end of every page there are quick feedback buttons:

- 👍 **Good quality** or 👎 **Bad quality** to rate it at a glance
- 🔄 **Update needed** if something looks out of date
- 💬 **Leave a note** to say what you'd change

We read them all, and they decide what gets fixed and extended next.
