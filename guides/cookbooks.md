# 📕 Cookbooks

A cookbook is a short, ordered recipe for one real problem: what you're trying to fix, what it looks like when it's fixed, and the steps to get there, each one with a worked example so you're never guessing what "do this" actually means in practice.

## What a recipe looks like

Every recipe opens with the problem it solves and what you'll have when you're done, then walks through the steps in order. Each step is one concrete action, often with a real worked example right there so you can see it done before you try it yourself.

## Finding one

On the Cookbooks page, the green **Latest** stripe at the top of the page lists the newest recipes, newest first. When you're signed in, each one says whether you haven't started it, are part way through, or have finished it.

Three ways in: the **Cookbooks** tab in the main nav, a **Cookbook** button on a roadmap step, and a cookbook banner on the matching cheat sheet. However you get there, you land on the same recipe.

## Working through one

Each step has a numbered circle beside it. Tap it to tick that step off, and the page remembers where you are, so you can leave partway through and pick up right where you left off. Already worked through the whole thing away from the screen? There's a **Mark all steps done** button under the last step so you don't have to tick them one at a time.

Every step also links back to the cheat-sheet row it came from, if you want the fuller explanation before moving on. The path along the top stays with you as you scroll, so you're always one tap from the cookbook, the category, or back out to everything.

Longer recipes are split into parts, and at the end of each part you'll see what you'll have working by then, so you can tell how far in you need to go for the bit you actually came for.

## How much you can try

Every cookbook gives you a real, full recipe or two to work through for free, no account needed to start. Past that, you'll still see what every remaining recipe covers, just not the steps themselves, until you subscribe.

## What you get for finishing

XP for every step and every recipe, a badge once you've worked through a whole cookbook, and a streak point for the day.

## Getting more detail

Stuck on a step, or want the reasoning spelled out? Tap **Explain** on the step, or on the whole recipe, for a plain-language walkthrough.

## How it ties to everything else

Recipes are built from our own **[cheat sheets](cheat-sheets.md)** and the real sources behind them, and a cookbook that's part of a **[roadmap](roadmaps.md)** shows up right there on the map. Your recipe progress earns **[XP](xp-and-levels.md)** and counts toward your **[streak](streaks.md)**, and your finished cookbooks and badges show up on your **[Stats](stats.md)** page.
