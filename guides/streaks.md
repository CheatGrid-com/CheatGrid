# Streaks 🔥

A daily streak is the simplest way to build a study habit, and it works across the whole app.

## How it works

You earn a **streak point on any day you study**, and there are several ways to get it:

- clear a **flashcard** session
- complete a **practice test**
- finish a **cookbook recipe**
- spend **20+ active minutes reading cheat sheets**
- spend **10+ active minutes exploring the [Concept Maps](concept-maps.md)**
- spend a few minutes reading **[Life Skills](life-skills.md)** pages

Reading and exploring time is counted per day, so it doesn't carry over from yesterday, and it only counts while you're really on the page. Only the first point counts each day, so you can't farm it, and any kind of studying keeps it going. Do a little every day and your streak climbs.

## Lives ♥♥♥

Real life happens, so you don't lose everything the moment you miss a day. You start each streak with **3 lives**. Miss a day and you spend a life instead of breaking the streak. Only when you run out of lives *and* miss another day does the streak reset (your best is always remembered).

**Hit a milestone, earn a bonus life.** Reach streak milestones (3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 days and well beyond) and you're awarded an extra life (up to a maximum of 10). The longer you keep going, the more cushion you build up, so a long streak gets harder to lose.


## Evening reminder

Busy days are when streaks end by accident, so the Streak page has an **Evening reminder** you can turn on. Pick an hour (evenings only), press **Turn on reminders** and allow notifications when your browser asks.

- You get **one** notification that evening, and only on days you haven't studied yet. Study first and nothing comes.
- It says what is actually at stake: whether a life would be used tonight, or whether your streak would end.
- **Send a test** shows you what it looks like; **Turn off** stops it on that browser.
- It works per browser, so turn it on on each device you want it on. On iPhone and iPad, add CheatGrid to your Home Screen first and turn it on from there.

## The Streak page

Open **Streak 🔥** from your account menu for the full picture:

- a flame showing your **current streak**, your **lives**, and progress toward your **next milestone**
- a **milestones** row that lights up as you reach each one
- quick stats (longest streak, total active days, this month) and a peek at your habits
- a **calendar and heatmap** so you can see every day you studied at a glance

## Share the streak

Past a certain length your streak comes with a **share card** and a little page to link to. It states the number of days and nothing else: no name, no profile, nothing that identifies you.

## A free topic, on us 🎁

Keep a **7-day streak** and you can unlock **one paid topic of your choice**, free and permanently: the cheat sheet, its flashcard deck and its practice test.

- Pick anything you like, including a certification.
- It's yours to keep. It doesn't disappear if your streak later breaks.

You'll see how many days are left on the Streak page, and the offer appears on any locked topic once you've earned it, so you can spend it on something you actually want.

## Tip

Pair it with a short daily **[flashcard](intelligent-flashcards.md)** Review. It's usually just a few minutes, and it's the easiest way to never miss a day.
